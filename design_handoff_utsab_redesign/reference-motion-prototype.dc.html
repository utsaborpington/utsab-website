Who we are

A home for Bengali culture in Orpington

UTSAB is a non-profit association celebrating Bengali and Hindu culture through Durga Puja, Saraswati Puja, and community gatherings throughout the year. We bring together generations to share prayers, food, music, and tradition — creating a home away from home.
