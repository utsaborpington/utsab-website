About UTSAB

A home away from home

UTSAB — meaning "celebration" — is a non-profit association committed to promoting Bengali culture and values through socio-cultural events like Saraswati Puja, Durga Puja, and Kali Puja. It is our humble endeavour that the younger generation appreciates the beauty of our rich cultural heritage and joins hands to ensure our cultural legacy continues to enthral the world.

Durga Puja transcends all barriers, creating for us a home away from home. Please join us in this magnificent celebration of life as part of the UTSAB family.

Our Purpose

Promote Hindu & Bengali practices — provide a platform for the community in the UK to engage in religious practices, festivals, pujas, and sacred rituals.

Create a spiritual and cultural hub — foster community through regular religious events, festivals, and cultural activities.

Strengthen Bengali identity — help younger generations stay connected with their heritage and its cultural significance.

Serve the community — offer spiritual guidance, community support, and welfare services to individuals and families.

Promote interfaith understanding — share Hindu and Bengali values with the broader community, encouraging respect and tolerance.

What We Do

Annual Festivals

Celebrating key Hindu and Bengali festivals such as Durga Puja, Saraswati Puja, and Poila Boisakh through community gatherings, prayers, processions, and feasts.

Educational Workshops

Classes and workshops for members — especially the younger generation — on Hindu scriptures, customs, traditions, and philosophy.

Charity & Outreach

Food drives, donations for the underprivileged, and support for local charitable initiatives, guided by the value of selfless service (Seva).

Cultural Programmes

Classical dance, music, and drama that celebrate Bengal's artistic heritage and bring the community together to showcase its traditions.

Donation & Sponsorship Terms

Utsab Bengali Association of Orpington ("the Association") is an unincorporated organisation that organises key Hindu and Bengali festivals such as Durga Puja, Saraswati Puja, Poila Boisakh (Bengali New Year), and others through community gatherings, prayers, processions, and feasts.

Purpose of the Association

The purpose of the Association is to promote Hindu and Bengali religious practices, create a spiritual and cultural hub, strengthen Hindu and Bengali identity — particularly among younger generations, serve the community, and promote interfaith dialogue.

Donations and Sponsorships

The Association accepts voluntary donations and sponsorships from individuals and corporate entities. All contributions are accounted for if paid to the designated bank account only, unless a written acknowledgement or receipt is issued for any cash payment.

All donations and sponsorships are accepted on the clear understanding that such payments are non-refundable after 48 hours of payment. Anyone seeking a refund must submit a written request, together with the bank transfer record or payment receipt, within 48 hours of payment.

Funds accumulated through donations and sponsorship fees are used to organise UTSAB's social and cultural events. All professionals and suppliers (excluding volunteers, who participate without remuneration) are paid from these funds.

Changes to Terms

These terms are subject to change from time to time. Donors and sponsors are advised to request the most up-to-date copy of these terms at the time of making payment.
